    5 Upgrading to Pro¶

    $29 per month. Cancel any time from the app.

    5.1 What Pro unlocks¶

    • 5,000,000 tokens / month (vs 50,000 on Free) — enough for hundreds of long copilot sessions.
    • Curated OpenFOAM knowledge base (RAG) — the copilot cites official OpenFOAM tutorials, foam-extend guides, and vetted forum answers.
    • Managed AI keys — no need to bring your own Gemini or Claude API key. Priority routing between Gemini 2.5 Pro and Claude Sonnet.
    • Priority email support at info@iterasim.in.

    5.2 How to upgrade¶

    • Click the account icon (top-right) → Pricing. Or type Ctrl+, and pick Plan & billing.
    • Click Upgrade to Pro on the Pro card.
    • Your default web browser opens on Stripe's secure Checkout page. Enter card details there — nothing sensitive is entered into Eddy itself.
    • After paying, close the browser tab and return to Eddy. Your account badge switches to PRO within a few seconds.

    Tip

    Stripe accepts most cards, Google Pay, and Apple Pay. Corporate cards and VAT numbers are supported — enter them in the Checkout form.

    5.3 Managing your subscription¶

    Once you are on Pro, the Pricing dialog shows a Manage subscription button. Clicking it opens Stripe's Customer Portal in your browser, where you can:

    • Update your card.
    • Download past invoices.
    • Cancel — takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep Pro until then.
    • Change the email that receives receipts.
