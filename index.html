    iteraSim Eddy — User Guide¶

    Navigating Possibilities in Fluid Dynamics

    iteraSim Eddy is a desktop application that drives OpenFOAM end to end — case setup, meshing, solver runs and post-processing — with an AI copilot across the whole workflow rather than bolted onto one step.

    This guide takes a new user from a blank machine to a finished, verified simulation. Everything here is the real application, screen by screen.

    The worked example¶

    If you only read one section, make it 4.6 Worked example — lid-driven cavity at Re = 1000.

    It is the standard CFD verification problem, it has a published answer, and it tells you whether a surprising result is your setup or the solver. A correct run reproduces Ghia, Ghia & Shin (1982) along the vertical centreline to well under one percent, with the primary vortex centred near (0.53, 0.56).

    Why start with a benchmark

    Running a case with a known answer first means every later surprise is information about your problem, not about your installation.
